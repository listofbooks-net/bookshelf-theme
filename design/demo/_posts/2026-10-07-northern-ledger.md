An inspector arrives in a frozen harbour town to audit a fish cannery and finds that the numbers balance perfectly. That is the problem.

Arvid's narrator is so dry that the jokes take a second to arrive. The mystery burns slowly, and the solution is hidden in plain sight on page forty.
