Novelist

Isobel Thorne

I write novels about small towns, old trades and the people who keep them going. My fourth book, The Bell Foundry, is out now.

I live on the edge of a fen with my husband, a lurcher called Pip and far too many notebooks. Before writing full time I restored church clocks, which explains a great deal.

The Bell Foundry

In a river town where the last bell foundry is about to close, a young caster is given one final commission: a bell that must ring for a wedding no one in the town wants to happen.
